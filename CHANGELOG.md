# Changelog

## 0.1 (2026-09-29)

The first release: Patchy 1.00, the open-source Photoshop-style image editor, as an app for every
Googlebook.

- **The whole editor:** PSD and PSB round trips with Photoshop, layers, masks, adjustment
  layers, layer styles, Smart Objects, editable text and vectors, pressure-aware brushes,
  filters, RAW photos and JavaScript scripting, all offline.
- **A desktop app on the Googlebook:** the menu bar sits in the window's title bar, menus open as
  you move along the bar, dialogs have proper frames, and the window stays sharp at the
  Googlebook's display scaling.
- **Files the Android way:** Open and Save go through the system file picker (Downloads, Drive,
  USB drives and other apps' storage); Open with and Share from other apps open images in Canvas;
  recent files and the status bar name documents by where they live.
- **Gestures:** pinch to zoom and two-finger pan, on the trackpad and on a touchscreen.
- **Appearance:** Dark by default, with Auto (follow the system) and Light one click away in the
  menu bar.
- **Print** through Android's print dialog (and Save as PDF).
- **Your work is kept:** when Android closes Canvas in the background, unsaved documents come
  back the next time you open it.
- **No internet permission,** no accounts, no tracking.

What's next: see the [to-do list](https://github.com/kuscher/canvas#to-do).
