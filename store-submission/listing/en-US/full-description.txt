Canvas is a desktop-style image editor for Android laptops, built on Patchy, the free and open-source editor by Seth A. Robinson. It opens and saves PSD and PSB files with their layers, and it works entirely offline.

Layers the way you know them
Layers with masks, blend modes and groups, adjustment layers and layer styles, Smart Objects, editable text and vector shapes.

Painting and editing
Brushes with pressure dynamics, selections, filters, gradients and patterns. Adjustments preview live on the canvas.

Your formats
PSD and PSB in and out with their layers, plus PNG, JPEG, WebP, TIFF, GIF, SVG, RAW photos and more. JavaScript scripting, palettes and seamless tiling come from Patchy too.

A real desktop window
The menu bar sits in the window's title bar next to the window buttons, menus follow the pointer, dialogs have proper frames, and everything stays sharp at any display scaling, maximised, restored or resized.

Your files, the Android way
Open and Save use the system file picker, so Downloads, Google Drive, USB drives and other apps' storage all work. Open with and Share from your files, photos or browser open images straight in Canvas.

Trackpad and touch
Pinch to zoom and pan with two fingers; a single finger still paints.

Nothing gets lost
If Android closes Canvas in the background, your unsaved documents come back the next time you open it. Print through Android's print dialog, including Save as PDF.

Dark by default, with Auto (follow the system) and Light one click away in the menu bar.

Private
No internet permission, no accounts, no ads, no analytics. Canvas sees only the files you open in it.
